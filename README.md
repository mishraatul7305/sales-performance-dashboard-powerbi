Just completed a Sales Performance Dashboard in Power BI!

I analysed ₹/$8.41M in sales across 17+ cities to understand profit, customer behaviour and payment trends.

🔹 KPIs: Total Sales, Profit (~7% of the sales/profit/unit-price split), Quantity
🔹 Quarterly distribution of orders (fairly balanced across Q1–Q4)
🔹 New vs Returning customers by city
🔹 Profit and order count by payment method
🔹 Order-level drill-down matrix

Tools: Power BI, DAX, Excel
